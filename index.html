DUBBE — AI video dubbing

        
AI / Technical Lead

        
Turns a lecture or course video recorded in one language into a dubbed version in another — and tells you which sentences to double-check. Every stage is a model, and errors compound, so DUBBE traces confidence through the whole chain and flags what a human should review.


        
    
          
  1. Video
  2. Speech recognition
  3. Sentences
  4. Translation
  5. Speech synthesis
  6. Timing
  7. Dubbed video
